How to read an entry

Primary Secondary Tertiary No placement
Primary
The color the ability is seen in most: highest volume, usually at the lowest rarity. It also tends to be the color that most often pushes the power level on effects that get pushed.
Secondary
Appears on a somewhat regular basis, but less often than Primary and not always at as low a rarity, and may carry restrictions.
Tertiary
Occasional: not every set, often narrow.
No placement
The 2021 article places this ability in none of that color's tiers.
Two placements
Left half: the article's color lists (“Mechanical Color Pie Lists”). Right half: its explanations (“All Abilities with Explanations”). This page shows both and does not choose.

These are categories, not scores: this page never adds them up or turns them into a number. Five slots, always W U B R G.

Compare color combinations computed

All 31 combinations of the five colors, at one size. Choose one to use it as your colors. Its one-color neighbors are outlined, and each step lists the exact entries whose placement is added, removed, or changes tier. These are counts computed from the 2021 placements, not statements by Rosewater, and a count is not a measure of strength.

Left and right arrows move through the entries in order; up and down move between rows. Enter opens an entry. Escape steps back.

Placements, entry names, sub-headings and explanations are Rosewater's, from the October 18, 2021 snapshot. Counts, best placements under selected colors and compass differences are computed by this page from those placements. Tiers are compared only within one ability, across colors; nothing is summed, scored or ranked.

Player search terms are navigation aids drawn from player research. They lead to article entries and are never Rosewater's wording. An absent 2021 placement is not evidence that no card of that color exists.
