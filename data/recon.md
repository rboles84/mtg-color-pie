## Notable

- **215 of 216** mechanics have at least one primary colour; **1 does not** — `Playing cards off the top of your library, paying their mana cost (You may look at the top card of your library and if it's a [blah] you may play it.)`, secondary in all five.
- **26 mechanics are available to all five colours** — the shared floor of the pie.
- **0 mechanics are tertiary everywhere they appear.** Occasional, not weak.
- Colorless has **no entries at all**: the Mechanical Color Pie is a *colour* document, so the colorless identity is empty here by definition, not by omission.
