## Scope and factual decisions

All 129 selected examples are released paper source cards through October 30, 2026. Actual source or mechanic-bearing face colors govern access, including indicators or characteristic-defining text when applicable. Costs, color identity, recipients and created tokens do not substitute for source colors. Digital, unreleased, playtest metadata, standalone tokens, Vanguard, acorn and silver-bordered objects are excluded from ordinary access. Ordinary cards in Planechase or Commander products are not excluded merely by their product type. Ordinary non-acorn Unfinity sources qualify. Roll to Visit Your Attractions retains its existing Special grouping; Reveal remains a generic rules reference with all six access slots not applicable and no selected card.

The selected notes were checked against complete saved Oracle text. Rapid Fire retains its before-blockers casting restriction and conditional rampage grant. Michelangelo, the Heart has the exact card-specific Raid (the Fridge) label, the attacked-this-turn condition, second-main-phase timing, a target creature counter and Food creation. This does not redefine Raid or claim that an unrelated release-notes page lists the card. Rally excludes Aligned Heart’s rally counters and Rally the Ancestors’ name-only match. Recruit excludes the name-only Freedom Fighter Recruit.

Grants retain their recipients and restrictions: Taigam requires its own attack and an instant or sorcery cast from your hand; Ian Chesterton grants replicate to Saga spells; Exterminate! pays replicate by tapping an untapped Dalek you control; Threefold Signal grants replicate {3} only to exactly three-color spells; Deeproot Historian grants retrace to Merfolk and Druid cards in your graveyard; Wrenn and Six supplies the red-green source for its −7 emblem grant. Playtest Jund ’Em Out is excluded by `promo_types`, even though its set type is masters. Riot’s Rhythm of the Wild grant is limited to nontoken creatures.

Reconfigure, Recover, Reinforce and Renew notes retain their actual costs. Rustic Clachan stays colorless despite its white reinforce cost. Renown preserves Aragorn’s attacking-creature grant. Revolt’s red Aether Revolt witness actually has the ability word. Ripple includes the colorless Thrumming Stone grant. Role token preserves the existing broad Role subtype scope and attributes all creation to the colored source. The selected Roll witnesses directly instruct the action; “Lifetime” Pass Holder’s payoff trigger does not.

Regenerate keeps all 16 observed source-color buckets. Rimebound Dead uses {S}; Ghost Ship costs {U}{U}{U}; Sentry of the Underworld also costs 3 life; Loxodon Hierarch sacrifices itself; Ragnar taps. Skeletonize remains red: it deals 3 damage and creates the black Skeleton with {B} regeneration only when a creature damaged that way dies that turn. Regeneration prevention, ability removal, standalone token objects and Vanguard Xantcha do not establish access.

## Evidence and provenance

The evidence ledger contains 44 request receipts: 38 successful saved raw responses, three original failed 404 attempts whose bodies were not retained, and three fresh reissues with actual saved 404 error bodies. No timestamp or hash is reassigned to a replacement body. The 38 successful responses consist of 32 complete discovery pages across all 23 entries and six targeted paper-printing responses. Regenerate follows both returned pages; Reveal follows all nine pages even though it does not receive access buckets. Every final discovery page ends without another page. The successful Read Ahead, Role token and Roll to Visit Your Attractions alternate queries correct the original encoded multiword searches; the original attempts are historical failed-request evidence only. All requests are GET, so no POST request body is omitted.

All 41 retained raw response bodies have actual URL, ordered UTC start/end timestamps, HTTP status and matching SHA-256 receipts. Retrieval occurred on October 31 UTC; the review date remains the client’s October 30 date. Every selected full-card object is embedded unchanged from a specific saved successful response, with its exact printing URL and raw-response path/hash. Hunding Gjornersen, Gabriel Angelfire, Marhault Elsdragon, Chromium, Ragnar and Lord of Tresserhorn bind to the targeted paper responses because the default discovery objects for those designs were MTGO-only printings. No artificial collection response was created.

The inherited canonical supplement supplies general definitions, rules and non-card references. Each record preserves its inherited reference/rules metadata separately from the fresh R receipts; no fresh article retrieval is invented. `selected-candidate-bindings.json` is explicitly labeled as a derived candidate index, not a raw API response or selected-card authority. Unselected hits are not supplied with individual notes or claimed individually reviewed; negative access statements stay bounded to the recorded search scope.
