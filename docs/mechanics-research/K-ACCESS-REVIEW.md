# K supplemental Color Access review

Protocol 2.0 review date: 2026-10-29. The canonical packet is
`k-protocol.json`; immutable Scryfall bodies and retrieval receipts are in
`k-protocol-evidence/`.

Kicker is represented by selected released-paper W/U/B/R/G and colorless
cards, plus WU, UB, and BG cards. Its screened family includes Kicker,
Multikicker, and Sticker kicker under CR 702.33: the Multikicker capture has
ordinary-paper monocolor, colorless, and WU examples already represented by
the selected map, while the nonacorn Unfinity Wicker Picker is a colorless
Sticker-kicker grant already represented by the selected colorless map. No
variant creates a new independent or combination bucket. Kinship is
represented by one released-paper W/U/B/R/G card. These are presence
witnesses, not a census or a hierarchy; unselected hits are not asserted as
individually reviewed.

Kinfall is deliberately narrower: Plane-Merge Elf is a green Mystery Booster
playtest card whose own trigger is labeled Kinfall. This is a source-card
specific factual description only. The accepted official review verifies card
existence, not an official general Kinfall definition, so no general rule or
mechanic claim is made.

Each selected card stores its full fresh Scryfall object and binds to the
capture that contains it. Receipts give request URL, time, status, and raw
SHA-256. Card links appear once in the selected access bucket; non-card
sources remain separate. Frozen 2021 content and earlier A–J evidence are
unchanged.
