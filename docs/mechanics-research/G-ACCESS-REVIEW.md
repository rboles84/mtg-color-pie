## Screening notes

- Gift is promised while casting. Instant and sorcery gifts happen before the spell's other effects on resolution; a permanent's gift comes from its entry trigger. Gift uses the spell or permanent's own colors; the offered Food, Fish, Treasure, or Octopus does not supply a recipient/token color. Digital Gift results are outside this ordinary-paper review.
- Goad includes direct actions and explicit grants from their source cards. The War Games and Rendmaw, Creaking Nest qualify through effects they create; their tokens' colors do not replace the source colors. Durations remain card-specific.
- Graft uses the source permanent's colors; Llanowar Reborn is a colorless graft witness despite producing green mana.
- Grandeur records its ability-word witnesses, including colorless Page, Loose Leaf. Gravestorm has selected blue and black spell witnesses. No missing bucket claims an impossible color.
