# J supplemental Color Access review

Protocol 2.0 review date: 2026-10-29.

This packet covers the four supplemental J records not mapped to the frozen
2021 calculation: Job Select, Join forces, Jump, and Jump-Start. It selects
one released-paper card for each independently documented color, colorless
bucket, or observed color combination. The canonical packet is
`j-protocol.json`; its raw Scryfall searches, the Cid release-notes capture,
and retrieval receipt manifest are in `j-protocol-evidence/`.

Job Select is represented in W/U/B/R/G and colorless. Join forces is
represented in W/U/B/R/G. Jump is the Cid, Freeflier Pilot card-specific
label, represented only by its white source; it is not the Jump-Start keyword.
Jump-Start is represented in blue and red plus blue-black, blue-red, and
five-color sources. Combination evidence does not imply a monocolor bucket.

The selected objects have their actual full Scryfall card facts and source
colors in the packet. The review excludes name-only references, recipients,
payoffs, and unrelated uses of “jump”; the bounded negative buckets say only
that no qualifying selected witness is represented in this packet.
