Official source capture — Now I Know My ABC's, Mark Rosewater, 2006-01-23.
URL: https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/making-magic/now-i-know-my-abcs-2006-01-23

The Bloodthirst section says Ravnica-block keywords had to line up with a guild. It identifies Gruul as red-green, built around attacking, and says that the attacking-focused design became bloodthirst. The article also cautions that later returns need not remain limited to the colors of their original guild.
