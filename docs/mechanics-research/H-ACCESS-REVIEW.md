## Action and source boundaries

Heal is the keyword action in rule 701.69: remove marked damage. The original Oracle search omitted reminder text. A fresh `fo:heal include:extras` follow-up finds explicit healing in regeneration reminders, supporting selected black, red, green, black-red and colorless witnesses alongside the direct red-green example and the static white regeneration of Knight of the Holy Nimbus. Rule 701.19 distinguishes creating a regeneration shield from its later replacement of destruction; the notes preserve that timing. Dr. McCoy is unreleased on this review date (November 13 release), so it cannot establish released-paper access. Time Stop mentions healing through the end-turn cleanup process, so it does not establish direct blue healing access. Healing Technique, Celestine and Old One Eye use names or flavor labels without the Heal action. Generic incidental rules processes do not become access merely by appearing in reminder text.

Harness is the rule 701.64 action making a permanent harnessed. This designation lasts until the permanent leaves the battlefield and has only the meaning other abilities assign it. The selected Stones use it for their infinity-marked abilities. Harnessed Lightning and Harness Infinity are name-only hits. Harmonize reduces only generic mana by the tapped creature's power and exiles the harmonized card when it leaves the stack. Hire a Mercenary is Black Market Connections' named mode: a 3/2 colorless Shapeshifter with changeling and 3 life lost, attributed to the black source.

Hidden Agenda has explicit Conspiracy scope. Heist has explicit digital Alchemy scope, including Triumphant Getaway's blue-black-red example. Its explanation is checked against a fresh capture of the original Wizards April 29, 2024 article: three random nonland cards, one exiled face down, permission to cast it while exiled using mana of any type. Hero's Reward uses Hydra Head only in labeled Face the Hydra / Challenge Deck scope. Its inherited archived Wizards design discussion remains identified as inherited and `definitionVerified=false`; the selected card does not turn that account into a universal trigger definition.

The saved Heroic four-color combined-character card and Horsemanship's Oddric are Mystery Booster 2 playtest cards, identified by `promo_types: playtest`. Special Heroes of the Realm, acorn, standalone token and digital objects do not enter ordinary-paper buckets. The selected Horsemanship white and blue witnesses use actual paper printing objects (Zhao Zilong and Lu Xun). Digital Jaheira/Raddic and special Urza's Hot Dog Stand do not add Hexproof-from combinations.

## Evidence and integrity

The fourteen original packet query receipts retain their original URLs, timestamps, HTTP statuses and actual hashes. Fresh full-Oracle, Wizards article and September 25, 2026 Comprehensive Rules captures have separate actual retrieval receipts in `repair-source-receipts.json`; the rules body matches the inherited canonical rules hash. Raw bodies retain original notation and are never normalized in place. Card facts are copied as full objects from their identified saved captures; player notes are individually authored paraphrases of the mechanic-relevant behavior.

`receipts.json` indexes the original packet requests and full-Oracle follow-up. A fifteenth original file, `heros-reward-discovery-1.json`, was an unused duplicate draft discovery. The original worker recovered its URL and HTTP 200, but never logged its retrieval UTC or original receipt hash. Those fields are explicitly unknown; a separately timestamped current SHA-256 integrity check is recorded without inventing provenance. This immutable unused file is not selected-card authority. Hydra Head resolves to the fully receipted `hero-s-reward-discovery-1.json` capture.

Not every unselected discovery hit is claimed individually reviewed. Negative results mean no example found in the reviewed scope. Existing A–G canonical packets, projected rows, source bindings and frozen 2021 content/calculations remain protected.
