# F Color Access review — October 28, 2026

## Scope and method

- Inventory: 29 F entries, including 25 supplemental targets. Fight, First Strike, Flash and Flying retain their frozen 2021 mappings. All 133 completed A–E projected records exactly match the starting commit `3e0a24d7c1b0bb5dc9e175935a38c88c5bba378d`; their source packets are unchanged.
- 24 access entries and one generic modal-choice preview reference, Face a dilemma. One verified card establishes presence, with one linked example per independent color, colorless bucket or combination. No ranks, counts-as-strength claims or impossible-color claims.
- 144 intentionally selected witnesses were read against saved card text. Their full objects match declared local captures, and selected source colors, dates, scopes and exact printing links were checked. Discovery captures are not an exhaustive individually reviewed card census.
- Existing local D/E sources, protocol, handoff, renderer and tests were reviewed first. New public Scryfall responses and official Star Trek pages retain raw bytes, request URLs, UTC timestamps, HTTP status codes and SHA-256 hashes. Extra unselected discovery captures retain their own receipts. Some initial queries were rate-limited; targeted follow-up and local saved evidence completed selection.
- Keyword indexes are discovery aids. Oracle-text follow-up recovered the black Frenzy Sliver grant, blue Scornful Aether-Lich fear ability and white-blue Ethereal Valkyrie foretell operation.

## Screening and presentation

- Ordinary paper excludes standalone tokens, digital, playtest, Unknown Event, silver-bordered and acorn material. Frenzy uses Frenzy Sliver, not Garbage Elemental. May of the Machine and Karlov’s Crossbow do not add ordinary Fateseal/Forecast colors.
- Ferocious uses the green Fanatic of Rhonas source; its standalone black token does not establish black access. Fire Sages replaces a standalone Dragon as the red Firebending witness.
- DFCs use the named mechanic-bearing face: The Core is colorless, Avatar Aang’s front is four-color, Avatar Roku’s saved back face is colorless, Ultra Magnus’s Armored Carrier face is white-red-green, and Sorin’s Food-making back face is white-black. No color is imported from the other face, mana costs, Commander identity, created token colors or activation costs.
- Forestwalk includes Jedit and Lord Windgrace as actual token creators. Food examples use their source creators’ colors. Academy Manufactor explicitly replaces token creation with creating a Food as well.
- Fuse examples describe the fused spell’s combined characteristics under saved rule 702.102b. Fifteen combination witnesses do not imply independent monocolor or colorless access. Unknown Event split cards stay excluded.
- Friends forever keeps seven multicolor combinations. Face a dilemma is a generic choice of modes, supported by the saved official mechanics article; payoff cards do not establish access. Star Trek remains labeled as preview material before its announced November 13 release.
- Brief mechanic explanations and factual card notes replace template copy. F’s dedicated symbol vocabulary covers its numeric, colored, X, tap and black-green hybrid costs. E and earlier rendering vocabularies remain unchanged. No dependency or theme changes.
