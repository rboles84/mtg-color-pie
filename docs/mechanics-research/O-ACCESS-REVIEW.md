## Scope and factual decisions

All selected examples are released paper cards through October 30, 2026. Source colors are taken from the mechanic-bearing card or face. Open an Attraction retains ordinary non-acorn Unfinity cards; no acorn-only material is used to infer access. Opus is described as a released-paper ability word; its namesake and other name-only results do not establish access.

Twelve raw discovery captures (the six original default-Oracle requests and six fresh full-Oracle requests), one selected-card collection and a fresh official Opus mechanics article are immutable. Every discovery response ends without another page. Full-Oracle screening includes reminder text and grants before source/face signatures are compared with the selected map. The receipt ledger records actual URLs, request timestamps, status and SHA-256 values. All 34 selected full objects are embedded from that collection, whose original name-based POST request is retained and hashed. All 14 response bodies and the POST request have actual SHA-256 receipts. Opus keeps the inherited supplement release-notes URL/verification date distinct from the newly retrieved official mechanics article; no fresh receipt is invented for the inherited reference. Negative wording is bounded to the recorded searches; unselected discoveries are not claimed individually reviewed.

Offering self-name matches (Volcanic Offering and Yawgmoth's Vile Offering), Opus name/Octopus matches (including Magma Opus), and Experimental Overload do not establish those mechanics. Arena-only Offspring/Opus/Overload designs and the Outlast sticker are outside ordinary-paper scope. The non-acorn Unfinity exception is explicit: those cards remain eligible despite the API's `funny` set type, while acorn cards and stickers remain excluded. Zinnia's grant supplies WUR; Arcus Acolyte has native and granted outlast in WG. Eldritch Immunity is actually colorless despite its costs, and its {4}{C} overload stays colorless.

Each selected note was written from the saved Oracle text: Offering names the offered subtype, Offspring/Overload retain complete mechanic costs, Outlast includes its full activation cost and tapping, and grants retain recipient restrictions. Open an Attraction examples describe direct entry/cast triggers or the optional opening mode, rather than inferring access from destroying Attractions. Opus notes describe the particular card's result. The assessment defines each mechanic once, then presents one selected linked card per bucket.

It also checks all 14 response hashes, the preserved request, all full selected objects, source/face signatures, complete costs, official/inherited Opus distinction, A–N row/binding parity, malformed-data isolation, safe links, keyboard behavior and font fallback. Independent QA remains separate.
