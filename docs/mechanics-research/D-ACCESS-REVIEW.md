# D Color Access review — October 28, 2026

## Decisions and evidence

- Scope: 27 supplemental D entries; mapped Deathtouch, Defender and Double Strike remain in the untouched 2021 catalog.
- One qualifying card establishes access. Show one linked witness per independent color, colorless bucket or combination; no strength judgments or P/S/T.
- Local A–C packets were checked first. Their overlap was limited, so targeted Scryfall Oracle searches supplied D discovery captures. Raw responses, request URLs, timestamps, HTTP status and SHA-256 receipts are retained under d-protocol-evidence/.
- The 141 selected record witnesses are individually checked against saved card text and face colors. Unselected discovery hits remain available locally; they are not an exhaustive reviewed/excluded card population.
- Keyword/native uses, direct actions, explicit grants and token creators qualify. Name-only text, reminder cards, planes, playtest/Unknown Event cards and support-only text do not. Ordinary paper and Arena/Conspiracy scopes are separate.
- Generic Double, Destroy and Discard stay rules references. Descend covers the existing ability-word family, not “descended this turn.” Devour includes devour artifact under the existing CR variant.
- Clara Oswald is colorless unless its pregame commander color-choice ability applies; the example says so explicitly. Devoid uses colorlessness rather than mana cost. Branch of Vitu-Ghazi independently supplies colorless Disguise; face-down status alone does not.
- Captured official Arena pages support spellbook choice and Double team’s nontoken restriction. Other non-card references are the existing verified supplement references, not newly fetched sources.

## Known discovery traps

- Boulder Dash and Chameleon, Master of Disguise are name matches, not the named abilities.
- Unknown Event and Mystery Booster playtest cards do not add ordinary-paper colors; this excludes the apparent green-white Demonstrate and red-green Discover results.
- Day/Night and face-down reminder cards are not source cards. Decayed token creators are valid; standalone token objects are not substitutes for source-card colors.
- The Descend search deliberately covers descend 4, descend 8 and fathomless descent. Generic “descend” or “descended” text is outside this entry.
- Drafting planeswalker abilities is not drafting a card from a spellbook.
